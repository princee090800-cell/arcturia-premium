        Questions
        
Before you book

      

      

        

          How much does a website cost?
          
It depends on the number of pages, content and features — a landing page and a full business website are different jobs. Book the free call, describe your business, and you will receive an exact written quote for your scope before any work or payment begins.

        

        

          How long does a website take?
          
The timeline is confirmed in writing with your scope, based on the size of the project and how quickly content (photos, text, logo) comes together. You will always know the current status — you speak directly to your developer.

        

        

          I already have a website. Can you redesign it?
          
Yes — choose "Website Redesign" in the booking form and mention your current website in the message. The first call covers what to keep, what to rebuild, and the exact cost.

        

        

          Will my website work on mobile and show on Google?
          
Yes. Every Arcturia website is mobile-first (most of your customers visit on phones) and includes Google-ready basics — page titles, descriptions and local structure for your area.

        

        

          How do customers contact me from the website?
          
WhatsApp chat, call buttons and a booking/enquiry path are built into every page — the same setup you are using on this page right now.

        

        

          Do you also help with real estate?
          
Yes — Arcturia also handles property enquiries in Vasai–Virar and the Mumbai region. Choose "Real Estate" in the booking form or message us on WhatsApp, and options will be matched and shared personally.

        

        

          What happens after I book?
          
Your booking opens in your WhatsApp or email, ready to send to Arcturia. We confirm your call, understand your goals, and send a written scope and price. You approve before anything moves forward — no obligation if it isn't right for you.
